Pairing with your PC…

Your PCs

    Control the soundboard on your PC from this device.

    1. On your PC, click the Resound tray icon and open Phone remote.
    2. Scan the QR code there, with this app or your camera.

    To install: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open Resound from your home screen and scan the code again there; the installed app keeps its own pairing.

    Can't scan?

    Point at the QR code on your PC
