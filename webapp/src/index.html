Up and running in a few minutes

  1. 1

    Install Resound on your PC

    One small program, no admin rights. The guided setup picks your speakers, adds your sound folders and sets up the virtual cable for voice chat.

  2. 2

    Scan the code with your phone

    Resound shows a QR code. Your camera opens this app, and it pairs with your PC on its own.

  3. 3

    Add it to your home screen, straight from the browser. It opens fullscreen, keeps the screen on and reconnects to your PC by itself.

Everything a soundboard needs

Built for playing sounds while you game or talk, without tabbing out.

Into voice chat and games

Sounds go to your speakers and through a virtual microphone (VB-Cable) to your friends, each with its own volume. Your real mic keeps working.

A remote in your pocket

Tabs, favorites, colours and emoji for every sound, custom layouts, progress bars, stop and pause. Works on phones, tablets and a second screen.

Your folders are your library

Drag folders in; each one becomes a tab. MP3, WAV, FLAC and OGG. Renaming or moving files keeps their settings.

Mic mute and push-to-talk

While a sound plays, Resound can mute your microphone and hold your game's push-to-talk key. "Talk through" lets you speak over it.

Hotkeys inside your game

Hold a key to pause the sounds and talk on another channel, or press one to stop everything at once.

Set up once, updated in a click

A guided setup installs VB-Cable for you. Updates and settings backups take one click each.

Why Resound

  • Private by design

    No account, no cloud, no telemetry, no ads. Your sounds never leave your PC.

  • Direct and encrypted

    Your phone talks straight to your PC over your own network, end-to-end encrypted.

  • Instant

    A tap reaches your PC within milliseconds over your Wi-Fi, and the sound starts right away.

  • Free and open

    Open source under the GPL-3.0. No store, no subscription, nothing to unlock.

How it works

This website only delivers the app. Everything after that happens between your phone and your PC.

A static app

The app is a set of files that your browser loads once from this site and keeps. Installed, it starts from your phone, even without internet, and only checks here for updates.

Pairing stays local

The QR code holds your PC's local address and a one-time code. They come after the # in the link, the part a browser never sends to any website.

A direct, encrypted connection

The app connects to your PC with WebRTC, the technology behind video calls, but without any server in between. The connection is encrypted and checked against your PC's certificate from the QR code.

Your data stays on your PC

Your sounds, library and settings never leave it. The phone only sends which button you pressed, and only phones you paired can connect.
