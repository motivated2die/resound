    Install Resound on your PC

    One small program, no admin rights. The guided setup picks your speakers, adds your sound folders and sets up the virtual cable for voice chat.

  2. 2

    Resound shows a QR code. Your camera opens this app, and it pairs with your PC on its own.

Everything a soundboard needs

Built for playing sounds while you game or talk, without tabbing out.

Into voice chat and games

Sounds go to your speakers and through a virtual microphone to your friends, each with its own volume.

A remote in your pocket

Tabs you swipe through and arrange, favorites, colours and emoji for every sound, custom layouts, progress bars, stop and pause. Works on phones, tablets or a second screen.

Any sound in a second

Search every tab at once, or filter by colour. Tap the search button or pull down with two fingers. Your sounds are only a second away.

Your folders are your library

Drag folders in so each one becomes a tab of sound effects. MP3, WAV, FLAC and OGG. Rename and move files at will, while Resound keeps track of things.

Import from sound button sites

Drag a sound from Myinstants or Sound Buttons World directly into your folders. Resound takes care of the import.

Every sound equally loud

Resound measures each sound once and evens out its loudness, so a whisper and a blaring airhorn play at the same level. Your own volumes still apply on top.

Mic mute and push-to-talk

While a sound plays, Resound can mute your microphone and hold your game's push-to-talk key. "Talk through" lets you speak any time.

Your favorites inside your game

One key slides your favorites in at the edge of the screen, without leaving the game. Pause and stop keys are there for when shit hits the fan.

Set up once, updated in a click

A guided setup installs VB-Cable for you. Updates and backups take one click each.

On your PC

See where your sound goes. No more guesswork.

Resound's PC controls draw your audio exactly as it flows: from your microphone and the soundboard to what you and your friends hear.

  • Every connection at a glance

    Connections light up with the signal and live meters show you what your friends will hear you before you join a call.

  • See interactions live

    A muted mic, push-to-talk keys held, playback paused: Resound shows it right on the cables as it happens.

  • Pinpoint problems with ease

    If something isn't connected or a microphone is muted, you'll see exactly what's wrong and where. Click it and Resound takes you to the fix.

Why Resound

  • Private by design

    No account, no cloud, no telemetry, no ads. Your sounds never leave your PC.

  • Direct and encrypted

    Your phone talks straight to your PC over your own network, end-to-end encrypted.

  • Instant

    A tap reaches your PC within milliseconds over your Wi-Fi, and the sound starts right away.

  • Free and open

    Open source under the GPL-3.0. No store, no subscription, nothing to unlock.

How it works

This website only delivers the app. Everything after that happens between your phone and your PC.

A static app

The app is a set of files that your browser loads once from this site and keeps. Installed, it starts from your phone, even without internet, and only checks here for updates.

Pairing stays local

The QR code holds your PC's local address and a one-time code. They come after the # in the link, the part a browser never sends to any website.

A direct, encrypted connection

The app connects to your PC with WebRTC, the technology behind video calls, but without any server in between. The connection is encrypted and checked against your PC's certificate from the QR code.

Your data stays on your PC

Your sounds, library and settings never leave it. The phone only sends which button you pressed, and only phones you paired can connect.

News

New Version 0.14

Your favorites inside your game, every sound equally loud

Press one key in any game and your favorites slide in at the edge of the screen. Scroll to a sound and press the key again to play it, or hold Esc to stop everything. Your game keeps the focus all along. Resound now also evens out the loudness of your sounds, so quiet clips and blaring airhorns play at the same level, and you can arrange your phone's tabs by dragging them.

Version 0.13

Import sounds from Myinstants and Sound Buttons World

Open the Sound library on your PC and drag a sound's name from either site onto one of your folders. Resound downloads it there, warns if you have it already, and lets you preview, rename or discard it right away. It shows up on your phone at once. Sound files from Explorer work too, and subfolders now become tabs of their own.

Version 0.12

Search all your sounds from your phone

The app can now search every tab at once: type part of a name, the letters in order or even a typo, and pick a colour to narrow it down. The redesigned app also brings a sliding tab bar that follows your swipes, a live preview of a button while you edit it, and help on screen when your PC can't be reached.

Version 0.10

Resound is now an app on your phone

The phone remote became an app you install from this page. It connects straight to your PC, encrypted, keeps the screen on, runs fullscreen and starts even without internet.
