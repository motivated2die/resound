    External editor

    Cut sounds with audiocutter.online

    Resound's editor is audiocutter.online, a third-party audio editor (not made by Resound), embedded in this window. When you continue, it loads from audiocutter.online and works on your file locally, in your browser: the file isn't uploaded, and getresound.app never receives it.

    Your browser may then ask whether this page may access apps on this device. Allow it: that's how the file gets from Resound into this window. You're asked here only once on this device.
