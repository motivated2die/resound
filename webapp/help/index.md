# Resound Help

How to set up, use and fix Resound, the soundboard for Windows that you control from your phone.

**New to Resound?** Start with [Install Resound](install.md), then [set up voice chat](voice-chat.md) and [connect your phone](connect-phone.md). The guided setup in Resound walks you through the same steps in about two minutes.

**Something not working?** The most common problems:

- [Others can't hear my sounds](others-cant-hear-sounds.md)
- [Sounds cut out or sound muffled in Discord](discord.md)
- [The phone can't connect to the PC](phone-cant-connect.md)
- [Push-to-talk or hotkeys don't work in a game](keys-in-games.md)
