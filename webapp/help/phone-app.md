# The phone app

The Resound app is your soundboard: tabs of buttons that play on your PC. Everything you change in it (favorites, colors, layouts) is saved on the PC, so all your devices show the same board.

## Play and stop

- **Tap a button** to play its sound. A bar fills the button while it plays. Sounds can overlap.
- **STOP** (bottom right) stops all sounds at once.
- **Pause and resume:** while something plays, a play/pause button appears in the top bar.
- **Talk-through:** hold the microphone button next to STOP. The sounds pause and your microphone is live, so you can say something; let go and they continue. Push-to-talk stays held meanwhile.

## Tabs

Each [sound folder](sound-library.md) is a tab at the bottom. Click on a tab, or swipe left and right over the buttons. **Favorites** is the first tab.

The app opens on **Favorites**, or, while you have no favorites yet, on your first tab. While it stays open, it keeps the tab you are on, also when it reloads for an update.

To arrange the tabs, enter edit mode (the pencil at the top), then long-press a tab and drag it. The order is saved for all devices.

## Search

Tap the magnifier at the top, or pull down on the buttons with two fingers. Search finds sounds in every tab, also with parts of words or a typo, and the colored dots filter by button color. Results play like any button.

## Customize a sound

Long-press a button, or enter edit mode and tap it, to open its settings:

- **♡ Favorite:** adds it to the Favorites tab and to the [in-game overlay](overlay.md).
- **Preview:** plays it on your PC's “You hear” device only, so only you hear it.
- **Custom Button Volume:** turns just this button up or down, for you and for others alike; the (i) next to it says so. The middle is the normal volume, and your default volumes on the PC stay as they are.
- **Button Color:** twelve colors, from the default dark grey and a light grey through red, orange, yellow, light green, green, teal, light blue, blue and purple to pink.
- **Button Emoji:** shows large in the button's background, or before the name in a list. Tap the emoji to change it, or **Delete** it.

Above the settings, the button shows how it looks with them, as a list row when the tab is a list. Tap it to preview.

## Layouts

In edit mode:

- the layout button at the top switches this tab between 3 columns, 2 columns and a list (one line per sound, in its color, with the emoji before the name);
- drag buttons to put them in your own order.

Tap ✅ to leave edit mode.

## App settings

The gear at the top opens the app's settings:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Keep Screen On | The screen doesn't turn off while the app is open (on by default) |
| Fullscreen | Hides the browser's bars; the installed app is fullscreen anyway |
| Vibrate on Tap | A short vibration on play and stop (Android) |
| Menu Buttons on the Left | Moves the top bar's buttons to the left |
| Reset | Page colours, Page layout, or All colours and layouts. Hold until it fills, then tap to confirm. Applies to all devices |
| PC | **Switch PC**, **Tour** (a short walk through the app), **Install the app** |

A long press on the Resound logo in the bottom bar opens or closes the panel on your PC.
