# Updates

## Resound on your PC

Resound checks for a new version shortly after it starts and then every 12 hours. When one is out, a dot and **Update** appear next to the version on the home page.

1. Click **Update**, or go to Settings → **App & startup**.
2. **What's new** shows the changes; **Update now** downloads the new version, checks it and installs it.
3. Resound restarts with all your settings, sounds and paired phones.

Prefer to check yourself? Turn off **Check for updates automatically** and use **Check now** when you like. Downloading a newer Resound.exe by hand and running it updates too.

## The phone app

The app updates itself. When you open it, it checks for a new version and switches to it as soon as that interrupts nothing: when the app is in the background, or you haven't touched it for a moment. Your pairing and settings stay.

The PC and the phone app work across versions. If a feature needs a newer Resound on the PC, the app tells you to update the PC.
