# Connect your phone

The Resound app runs in your phone's browser and can be installed from there, no app store needed. It talks to your PC directly over your network.

## Pair with the QR code

1. On the PC, open the panel and click **Web app** (or go to the guided setup's last step).
2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera and open the link. The Resound app opens and pairs with the PC by itself.
3. That's it: your sound folders appear as tabs.

Your phone must be on the **same Wi-Fi** as the PC: not mobile data, not a guest network, not a VPN. The PC itself may be on a cable. Each code works once and for 10 minutes; Resound shows a new one after every pairing.

:::note Nothing goes through the internet
The QR code holds your PC's local address and a one-time code. After loading the app once, your phone connects straight to the PC, encrypted. Your sounds never leave the PC.
:::

## Install the app

Installed, Resound starts from your home screen, opens fullscreen, keeps the screen on, and starts even without internet. Tap **Install app** at the top of the soundboard, or Settings → **Install the app**. How it works depends on the browser:

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet) | Tap **Install** in Resound's install sheet |
| Android (Firefox) | Menu (three dots) → **Install** |
| iPhone and iPad | **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** |
| Mac (Safari) | **File** → **Add to Dock** |

:::warning iPhone and iPad: pair again in the installed app
iOS keeps the installed app separate from Safari. Open Resound from your home screen and scan the PC's code again there, with **Scan QR code** in the app.
:::

## More devices

- **Several phones or tablets:** pair each one the same way. They share favorites, colors and layouts, which are stored on the PC.
- **This PC:** the button in the top right corner of **Web app** on the home page opens the app in a phone-sized window on the PC, for example on a second or touch screen.
- **Several PCs:** pair with each. In the app, Settings → **Switch PC** lists them; ✕ forgets one.

## Sign out devices

The PC's Remote App page lists every paired device, such as “Chrome on Android”, with when it was last used. **Remove** signs out just that device; **Sign out all devices** signs out every one. A signed-out device is disconnected at once and has to scan a code again.

Not connecting? See [The phone can't connect](phone-cant-connect.md).
