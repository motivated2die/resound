# Import sounds from websites

Drag a sound from a sound button website onto one of your folders in the panel, and Resound downloads it there. No account needed.

## From Myinstants or Sound Buttons World

1. On the PC, open Settings → **Sound library**, or click **Folders** on the home page. You need at least one [sound folder](sound-library.md).
2. Click **Myinstants** or **Sound Buttons World**. The site opens in your browser; the panel stays open beside it.
3. Find a sound and **drag its name** onto a folder in the panel. On Myinstants, the small link icon under a button works too.
4. Resound downloads the sound and opens a card under the folder:
   - the **name**, taken from the website and selected, so you can type a new one;
   - **Preview** plays it to you only;
   - **Save** (or <kbd>Enter</kbd>) keeps the name you typed;
   - **Discard** deletes the downloaded file.

The sound is on your phone right away. If you press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or start the next import, it keeps the website's name.

## Other websites and files

You can also drop:

- a link to a sound file (MP3, WAV, FLAC or OGG) from any website, or a link to a page with exactly one sound;
- sound files from Explorer or your browser's download list: they are copied into the folder, the originals stay where they are.

A folder dropped anywhere else on the page becomes a new library folder.

## Cut a sound from a video

Drop a video (MP4, MKV, WEBM, MOV and others) or audio Resound can't play as it is (M4A, AAC, OPUS, WMA, AIFF and others) onto a folder. While you drag it over the folder, the folder reads **Drop to cut in the editor**.

1. A browser window opens with the file loaded in [the audio editor](audio-editor.md).
2. Select the part you want and press **Add to Resound**. The clip is saved into the folder, and the usual card opens in the panel to name it.
3. Cut as many clips as you like; each **Add to Resound** saves one more. Press **Done** at the top of the window when you're finished: it closes.

[The audio editor](audio-editor.md) explains it all: the first-time notice, supported formats, trimming sounds already in your library, privacy and what to do when something goes wrong.

## Already in your library?

Resound checks every new sound against your library. If you have it already, the card says where. You can still keep it, or discard it.

## When an import fails

| Message | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No sound file found at this link | Search and category pages hold many sounds without a direct link | Drag a single sound's name, or open its own page and drag the address from there |
| This page has several sounds | Resound only takes a page with exactly one sound | Drag the one sound's name or link |
| Not a supported sound format | Not MP3, WAV, FLAC or OGG, or the file is damaged | Download it in your browser and drop the file onto a folder to [cut it in the editor](#cut-a-sound-from-a-video) |
| The sound file is too large | Over 25 MB | Shorten it, or copy the file in from Explorer |
| Links to this PC or the local network are not downloaded | Resound only downloads from the internet | Copy the file into the folder in Explorer |
| Drop the sound onto one of the folders | It landed beside the folder fields | Drop it right onto a folder |

:::note About the sounds
Sounds on these sites are uploaded by their users, and many are clips of copyrighted material. Resound only downloads what you drag in, one sound at a time, like a browser download.
:::
