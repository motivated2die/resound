# Launchpad

Play any button on your phone with your keyboard, without taking your hands off it: hold one key, and your phone shows a key on each button of the tab it shows. Press a button's key to play it. You can try it on the [Resound website](/#launchpad).

## Turn it on

1. Settings → **Launchpad** → **+** on the **Launchpad** card, and press a key, a combination or a mouse button (3–5), for example <kbd>Caps Lock</kbd> or Mouse 4. Pick one you don't need in your games: while you hold it, your game doesn't get it.
2. Open the Resound app on your phone (or on a second screen) and leave it on the tab you want to play from.

## Use it in a game

| Do this, holding your launchpad key | And |
|---|---|
| Look at your phone | Each button shows its key: <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd>, <kbd>0</kbd>, then <kbd>A</kbd> to <kbd>Z</kbd> |
| Press a button's key | That sound plays for everyone, as if you tapped it. Press again to play it again |
| Press <kbd>Space</kbd> | All sounds stop (or pause, see [Space](#space)) |
| Press the arrow keys or turn the mouse wheel | The next or previous tab opens, on your phone too |
| Let go of the launchpad key | The keys disappear, and your keyboard goes back to your game |

The keys follow the buttons as your phone shows them, in its layout and sorting, from the top left: the first button is <kbd>1</kbd>, the tenth <kbd>0</kbd>, the eleventh <kbd>A</kbd>. A tab can have up to 36 keys; buttons after the 36th have none. The numbers on the numpad work like the ones above the letters.

While you hold the key, the phone's talk button turns into a yellow launchpad button, the logo and the connection dot turn yellow, and the bottom bar and the open tab glow yellow, so you can tell it's on.

:::note
While you hold the launchpad key, **none** of your key presses reach your game: not just the keys on the buttons, but every key you press. Keys you were already holding before, like <kbd>W</kbd> to walk, carry on as usual. Mouse clicks always go through.
:::

## Which phone shows the keys

Each open Resound app tells your PC which tab it shows. The keys show on the app you **used last**: tapped, scrolled or switched tabs on. With two phones (or a phone and a window on your PC), touch the one you want to use. If you switch tabs on the phone while holding the key, the keys follow.

Without a phone, the launchpad still plays the tab your phone showed last, Favorites at first. The badge on your screen tells you which tab that is.

## Space

Under **Space** on the **Launchpad** page, choose what <kbd>Space</kbd> does:

- **Space to stop** (the default): a tap stops all playing sounds.
- **Space to pause**: Space works like the pause button on your phone:


  - **Tap** <kbd>Space</kbd>: all sounds pause. Tap again: they carry on.
  - **Hold** <kbd>Space</kbd> for a moment (0.7 seconds): all sounds stop.

## The badge on your screen

While you hold the key, a round yellow badge shows on your screen, with the tab your keys play beside it. When you press a key, the sound's emoji and name show there for a moment, the badge flashing green (red if the sound couldn't play).

On the **Launchpad** page you can turn off the sound names, the tab's name, or the whole badge, and pick where it shows: a corner, or the middle of the top or bottom edge (the top middle by default). Like the [in-game overlay](overlay.md), it never takes the focus from your game.

## A different key in one game

A game can have a launchpad key of its own, or no launchpad at all, like any other [keybind for one app](push-to-talk.md#keybinds-for-one-app). Click **Set it in App keybinds** below the key on the launchpad's page, open the game (or add it), and use the **Launchpad** card there: **+** gives it its own key, **⊘** turns the launchpad off in that game, **↺** brings back the global key.

## Limits

- Each key does one thing: the launchpad key can't also be the overlay key, a pause or stop key, or a push-to-talk key.
- If another window comes to the front, or Windows shows a security prompt, while you hold the key, the launchpad ends: press the key again.
- Games running as administrator don't let Resound see your keys. See [Keys don't work in a game](keys-in-games.md).
- To turn the launchpad off, remove its key with **✕** on its page.
