# In-game overlay

Play your favorites without leaving the game: one key slides a bar of your favorite sounds in at the edge of the screen. You can try it on the [Resound website](/#overlay).

## Turn it on

1. Settings → **In-game overlay** → **+** on the **Overlay** card, and press a key, a combination or a mouse button (3–5), for example <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>F1</kbd> or Mouse 4.
2. Choose the **Screen edge**: Left (default) or Right.
3. Click **Show the overlay** to try it right there, without a game.

The bar shows your [favorites](phone-app.md#customize-a-sound), in the order of the phone's Favorites tab. No favorites yet? It tells you how to add some.

## Use it in a game

| Do this | And |
|---|---|
| Press your key | The bar slides in |
| Turn the mouse wheel | The highlight moves through your favorites |
| Press your key again | The highlighted sound plays for everyone |
| Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> | The bar closes |
| Hold <kbd>Esc</kbd> | The bar turns red and closes, and all sounds stop |

The highlight stays where you left it, so you can pick a sound ahead of time and later play it with two presses.

Your game keeps the focus all along. While the bar shows, only your key, <kbd>Esc</kbd> and the mouse wheel are kept from the game; everything else, mouse clicks included, goes through.

## Limits

- The overlay shows on the main screen, over games in windowed, borderless and most fullscreen modes. A game in true “exclusive” fullscreen covers it: switch the game to borderless or windowed.
- Games running as administrator don't let Resound see your keys. See [Keys don't work in a game](keys-in-games.md).
- To turn the overlay off, remove its key with **✕** on its page.
