# Backup and restore

A backup holds all your settings in one file: audio devices and volumes, sound folders, push-to-talk and other keys, favorites, sound volumes, colors and emoji, phone layouts, and your paired phones. Your sound files are not included; they stay in their folders. To keep a copy of them too, [zip them](#zip-your-sound-files).

## Back up

Settings → **Backup & restore**:

- **Back up now** saves a backup in Resound's backup folder.
- **Save as…** saves one wherever you like, for example on another drive or in a cloud folder.

:::warning Uninstalling deletes the backup folder
Backups made with **Back up now** sit next to Resound's settings, and [uninstalling](uninstall.md) removes them. Keep the ones you need with **Save as…** somewhere else.
:::

## Restore

1. Choose a backup from the list and click **Restore**, or **Restore from file…** for one saved elsewhere.
2. Confirm. Resound restarts with the settings from the backup.

Your current settings are saved as a backup first, so a restore can be undone the same way.

## Zip your sound files

Settings → **Backup & restore** → **Sound files** → **Zip all sounds…** puts all your sound folders into one zip file: each folder with all its subfolders, empty ones too, and every sound in its place.

1. Resound tells you how many sounds that is and about how large the zip gets. It's a full copy of your sounds, so it can be large: it needs that much free space where you save it.
2. Click **Save as…** and choose where the zip goes, for example another drive or a cloud folder.
3. Resound shows its progress; you can close the panel meanwhile, or **Cancel**. When it's done, **Show in folder** opens it in Explorer.

Resound doesn't restore this zip. To use your sounds again, unzip it and add the folders under **Sound library**.

## Move to a new PC

1. On the old PC: **Save as…**, and copy the backup and your sound folders (or [a zip of them](#zip-your-sound-files)) to the new PC.
2. On the new PC: [install Resound](install.md) and choose **Restore from a backup…** on the guided setup's welcome page.
3. If your sound folders are in a different place now, add them again under Sound library. On your phones, scan the new PC's code once, so they learn its address.
