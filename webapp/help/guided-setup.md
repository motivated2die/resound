# The guided setup

On its first start, Resound opens a short setup in its panel: four steps, about two minutes. You can skip any step with **Skip for now** and do it later in Settings.

| Step | What you do | Later under |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Add your sounds | Drag folders with sound files onto the panel, or choose them | Settings → Sound library |
| 2. Audio and voice chat | Choose your microphone and where you hear sounds, install VB-Cable, connect it | Settings → Audio & voice chat |
| 3. Push-to-talk key | Set the key your game uses for push-to-talk, if it uses one | Settings → Keybinds |
| 4. Connect your phone | Scan the QR code; optionally start Resound with Windows | Home page → Web app |

Each step has its own article: [sound folders](sound-library.md), [voice chat](voice-chat.md), [keybinds](push-to-talk.md) and [your phone](connect-phone.md).

## Before you start

If Soundux or Soundpad is set up on your PC, the welcome page shows a card for each, above the setup's buttons. Its import carries your sounds and settings over; you choose what, and the steps then show it for you to check. See [Switch from Soundux](soundux.md) and [Switch from Soundpad](soundpad.md).

Below the cards:

- **Start setup**: the four steps.
- **Skip, I'll set it up myself**: goes straight to the home page.
- **Restore from a backup…**: brings back settings from an earlier installation, see [Backup and restore](backup.md).

## Interrupted?

Resound remembers the step you were on. If you close the panel, quit, or restart Windows (VB-Cable needs a restart), the setup continues where you left it.

## Run it again

Settings → **Run the guided setup again** (at the bottom). Your settings stay; the setup just walks through them again.
