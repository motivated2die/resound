# Contributing to Resound

Thanks for your interest in Resound!

**I don't accept pull requests from outside contributors at the moment.** Pull requests will be closed without review.

Suggestions are very welcome, though, as issues:

- **[Report a bug](https://github.com/motivated2die/resound/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**: the form asks for your version and the log, and explains where to find it.
- **[Suggest a feature](https://github.com/motivated2die/resound/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)**: describe what you'd like to do; a 👍 on an existing issue counts too.

Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

To build Resound yourself, see [docs/BUILDING.md](docs/BUILDING.md) and [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md). Resound is licensed under the [GNU General Public License v3.0](LICENSE).
